GCSE German Writing Coach

Choose a Question 2 or Question 3 task, write your answer in German, then get instant feedback on bullet-point coverage, word count, time frames, complexity and likely accuracy issues. This is a revision coach, not an official examiner. It gives best-fit estimates to help pupils improve their writing.

What the checker looks for

It checks whether the main bullet points are addressed, whether the answer is near the recommended length, whether time frames are present, whether there is some complex language, and whether there are obvious issues with common German patterns such as weil/wenn word order, modal + infinitive, tense formation, verb agreement and sentence capitalisation. This version is deliberately strict.
